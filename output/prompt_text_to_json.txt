SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Accomplished Herbology scholar, internationally recognised experimental botanist, and decorated veteran of the Second Wizarding War. Professor of Herbology at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, holding the position continuously since 2000. Led Dumbledore's Army resistance operations under Death Eater occupation at age seventeen, coordinated the defence of Hogwarts castle, and personally destroyed Voldemort's final Horcrux. Research focus on magically-responsive plant cultivars, medicinal herbological applications, and the conservation of endangered wizarding flora. Former Auror. Gryffindor, through and through.

Jan 2004 – Present
A multi-institution conservation programme aimed at cataloguing, seed-banking, and cultivating endangered magical plant species. Partners include the Royal Botanic Gardens at Kew, the Beauxbatons Botanical Institute, and two Scandinavian magical natural history museums.

Battle of Hogwarts Memorial Garden
Jun 1999 – May 2001
Designed, planted, and oversaw the establishment of the permanent memorial garden in Hogwarts's east grounds, commemorating those killed in the Battle of Hogwarts. Each planting was selected for symbolic resonance with the qualities of those remembered.
  • Designed a planting scheme representing 51 individual fallen defenders, each with a named species chosen in consultation with their families
  • Personally planted and maintained all specimens until full establishment
  • Installed enchanted permanent-bloom charms on five central specimens, designed to flower year-round in perpetuity
  • The Garden has since become an annual pilgrimage site on the anniversary of the Battle, maintained by Hogwarts students as a service project
