SUMMARY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open-minded and exceptionally perceptive magizoologist with expertise in rare, disputed, and misclassified magical creatures. Contributor and assistant editor at The Quibbler, with a particular focus on cryptofaunal investigation and Wrackspurt phenomenology. Decorated member of Dumbledore's Army and veteran of the Battle of Hogwarts. Widely regarded as one of the most original thinkers in contemporary British wizarding natural philosophy. Comfortable operating in remote and hostile environments. Possesses an unusually high tolerance for social scepticism.

Operation Snorkack — Northern Sweden Expedition
Jun 2005 – Sep 2005
A three-month solo field expedition to northern Sweden to document the habitat and behavioural patterns of the Crumple-Horned Snorkack. Utilised a proprietary passive observation platform and a novel sympathetic-magic tracking system developed over two years of preparation.
  • Obtained the first verified photographic and pensieve-archived evidence of a living Crumple-Horned Snorkack
  • Mapped a 40-square-kilometre habitat range and documented seventeen discrete behavioural observations
  • Submitted formal evidence package to the Ministry's Beast Division; review ongoing
  • Survived an unexpected confrontation with a territorial Erkling in week six with no lasting injuries

The Nargle Reclassification Project
Apr 2002 – Dec 2004
Systematic multi-site field study of Nargle colony behaviour in Welsh mistletoe groves, designed to provide sufficient evidence for a formal Ministry reclassification from 'unclassified' to 'documented species'.
  • Identified seventeen distinct colony sites across eight counties
  • Documented seasonal behaviour patterns, nesting habits, and Nargle-mistletoe symbiotic relationship
  • Successfully petitioned for reclassification — the first new creature formally added to the Ministry's Class II list in eleven years
  • Peer-reviewed publication in the Journal of Magical Natural History cited 43 times within five years
