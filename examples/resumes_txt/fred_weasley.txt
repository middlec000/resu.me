Skiving Snackbox — Full Product Line
Sep 1993 – Apr 1996
A line of dual-action sweets that induce convincing illness symptoms (nosebleeds, fever, fainting, vomiting) upon eating one end, with an antidote built into the other end. Developed over three years of iterative testing — primarily on Fred himself — to achieve the correct symptom intensity, duration, and antidote reliability.
  • Achieved 100% symptom induction reliability across all eight Snackbox varieties
  • Calibrated antidote response time to under 90 seconds in all weather conditions and age groups
  • Successfully passed through confiscation attempts by Filch and Umbridge by disguising products as standard sweets
  • Generated estimated 70 Galleons in pre-launch orders from within Hogwarts alone before the shop opened

Extendable Ears — Listening Device
Jun 1994 – Aug 1995
A flesh-coloured listening device capable of being extended under doors, through keyholes, and around corners at distances up to twenty feet. Uses a sympathetic vibration enchantment to relay sound to a receiver end held by the listener.
  • Overcame Ministry-grade Anti-Eavesdropping Charms through a novel sympathetic resonance technique developed independently
  • Product adopted by the Order of the Phoenix for intelligence-gathering operations
  • Sold out within four days of the Diagon Alley shop opening
  • Second-generation version extended maximum range to 30 feet and added a muffling filter for background noise

Weasley's Wildfire Whiz-Bangs — Advanced Fireworks
