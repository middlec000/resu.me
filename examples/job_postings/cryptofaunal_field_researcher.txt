ABOUT THE ROLE

The International Confederation of Wizards' Wildlife Field Research Unit
is expanding its Cryptofaunal Investigation Programme and seeks an
exceptional Field Researcher to undertake multi-week expeditions across
Northern Europe and Scandinavia to document magical species with disputed
or unresolved classification status.

This is not a desk job. The successful candidate will spend significant
portions of the year in the field, often in remote locations, working
independently with minimal supervision to gather observational data,
photographic evidence, and physical samples for formal submission to the
ICW Species Classification Board.

We are specifically looking for someone who does not dismiss a creature
simply because the Ministry has not officially acknowledged its existence.
Our current classification backlog includes over forty species held in
'unresolved' status, several of which have been there since 1842. This is
not acceptable and we intend to do something about it.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Conduct independent field expeditions to document cryptofaunal
    species, with particular focus on Scandinavia, Scotland, and
    the Welsh Highlands
  • Gather and prepare evidence packages for Species Classification Board
    review, including observational notes, photographs, Pensieve archives,
    and physical samples where ethically obtainable
  • Develop and deploy passive observation methodologies that minimise
    disturbance to target species
  • Maintain accurate field journals and submit expedition reports within
    four weeks of return to base
  • Liaise with local magical naturalists and community contacts to
    identify sighting records and historical habitat data
  • Present findings at the annual ICW Cryptofaunal Symposium
  • Mentoring of Research Assistant (post shared between two researchers)

ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Minimum Class II Field Magizoologist Licence
  • Demonstrated experience in independent field research and species
    documentation, evidenced by published work, formal reports, or a
    portfolio of field observations
  • Strong practical knowledge of magical creature tracking, passive
    observation, and ethical capture-and-release protocols
  • Willingness to work in remote and occasionally hazardous environments
    for extended periods
  • Excellent written reporting skills
  • Physical fitness sufficient for extended wilderness fieldwork

DESIRABLE

  • Specific experience with disputed-classification or 'unclassified'
    species — candidates who have already done fieldwork on species the
    Ministry pretends don't exist are strongly preferred
  • Published or submitted work in a relevant field
  • Conversational Swedish, Norwegian, or Welsh for local liaison
  • Experience with Nundu-proximate environments (SAFETY CERTIFIED ONLY)
  • Background in magical journalism or science communication to support
    public engagement work
  • High tolerance for being told that what you saw 'probably doesn't
    exist' by officials who weren't there

WHAT WE OFFER

  • 44 Galleons per week
  • All expedition costs covered including travel, accommodation,
    specialist equipment, and emergency medical portkey insurance
  • Two research assistants shared across the unit
  • Access to the ICW's restricted archive of historical cryptofaunal
    sighting records — including several not available to the public
  • Annual budget for specialist field equipment
  • Generous annual leave (22 days base + up to 15 additional field
    acclimatisation days per year)
  • Strong institutional backing for Classification Board submissions —
    we have three sitting Board members and we are not afraid to use them

We would like to be transparent: this role will at times involve
presenting evidence that is contested by people with institutional power.
Applicants should be comfortable holding their position calmly when
confronted by scepticism from officials, academics, or colleagues who
have made up their minds in advance. Resilience in the face of being
thought a bit odd is considered an asset, not a liability.
