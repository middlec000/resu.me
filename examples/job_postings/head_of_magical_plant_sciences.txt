ABOUT ST MUNGO'S

St Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries is Britain's
foremost provider of wizarding medical care, operating continuously since
1600. We treat over 40,000 patients annually across six specialist wards
and employ more than 300 Healers, Potion-makers, and support staff.
Following a comprehensive internal review of our treatment protocols, we
are establishing a new Magical Plant Sciences Department and seek an
exceptional founding Director to build it.

ABOUT THE ROLE

This is a Director-level appointment and the founding position for a new
clinical support department. The Head of Magical Plant Sciences will be
responsible for building, from the ground up, an in-house botanical
cultivation and supply capability that reduces our dependence on external
apothecaries and commercial suppliers for critical medicinal plant
material.

The post-war period has exposed significant vulnerabilities in our
supply chain for several critical plant-based treatments, including
Mandrake restorative preparations and several venomous-exposure antidotes.
We need someone who can grow what we need, at the quality we require,
when we need it — and who can train a team to do the same.

This is a genuinely important job. Lives depend directly on the quality
and availability of plant-based treatments at this hospital. We are
looking for someone who understands that and is ready for the weight of it.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Establish the new Magical Plant Sciences Department, including
    securing and fitting out dedicated greenhouse space, hiring staff,
    and setting up operational systems from scratch
  • Design and oversee cultivation programmes for all plant species used
    in St Mungo's apothecary operations, prioritising those critical to
    emergency and long-term patient care
  • Develop quality assurance standards for internally cultivated plant
    material and oversee compliance with Ministry of Magic guidelines on
    clinical-grade botanical ingredients
  • Lead research into improved cultivation, maturation, and preparation
    methods for key medicinal plant species — particularly Mandrakes,
    Dittany, and Gillyweed-derived compounds
  • Liaise with the Potions-Based Treatment Division on ingredient
    specifications and substitution protocols during supply shortfalls
  • Establish supplier relationships for species that cannot be grown
    in-house, with appropriate quality verification procedures
  • Represent the department at St Mungo's Board level and contribute
    to hospital-wide strategy
  • Supervise and develop a department of up to eight botanical staff

ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Advanced qualification in Herbology or Magical Botany, ideally to
    N.E.W.T. Outstanding level or post-graduate equivalent
  • Demonstrable experience cultivating and preparing clinical-grade or
    high-value magical plant material
  • Level 3 Magical Hazardous Plant Handling certification, Class IV
    (Carnivorous and Venomous)
  • Experience managing or leading a team
  • Genuine expertise with Mandrake cultivation and restorative
    preparation — candidates should be able to speak to this in detail
    at interview
  • Strong interpersonal skills for working with Healers, Potions staff,
    and hospital administrators

DESIRABLE

  • Published or ongoing research in magical plant cultivation or
    applied Herbology
  • Experience establishing or significantly expanding a greenhouse
    facility
  • Background in or knowledge of clinical-grade ingredient standards
  • Familiarity with endangered species conservation issues affecting
    supply chain resilience
  • Prior experience working with or supporting a hospital, hospice,
    or long-term care environment

We note that this post involves regular close contact with the hospital's
Long-Term Residential Ward, and the successful candidate will be expected
to handle that with sensitivity. Personal connection to the work of
long-term patient care — in whatever form — would be welcomed.
