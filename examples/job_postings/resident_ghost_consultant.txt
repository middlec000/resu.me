ABOUT ENCHANTED ESTATES

Enchanted Estates Ltd is Scotland's oldest specialist magical property
consultancy, established 1712, with a portfolio of over 200 historic
wizarding properties ranging from Highland manor houses to Edinburgh tower
residences. We provide surveying, valuation, and historical research
services to buyers, sellers, and the Wizarding Heritage Trust.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We are seeking a Ghost Consultant to provide expert advisory services
across our property portfolio on matters of spectral occupancy, structural
memory, and historical witness verification. This is a contracted retainer
position — not a permanent residency — and requires a ghost of flexible
disposition willing to travel between properties as needed.

The role is primarily one of professional consultation. Property buyers
frequently wish to understand who else, besides themselves, will be living
in a given estate. Sellers sometimes need to negotiate with existing
ghostly residents before a sale can complete. Historic properties often
contain architectural features, hidden passages, and subsurface chambers
that are known to their spectral inhabitants and nobody else.

We need someone who knows this world well, communicates clearly, and can
discuss difficult or upsetting historical events in a way that is
informative without being unnecessarily traumatic for prospective buyers.
There is a meaningful distinction between 'this property has significant
paranormal heritage' and 'I will now describe my death in graphic detail
during the viewing.' We need someone who understands that distinction.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Conduct spectral occupancy assessments for properties in our portfolio
    prior to sale or rental, identifying any existing ghost residents and
    advising on their temperament, territorial claims, and likely response
    to new occupiers
  • Serve as a witness and interpreter in disputes between living property
    owners and ghost occupants — such disputes are more common than you
    might expect and the legal framework is surprisingly complex
  • Provide historical testimony on properties where your knowledge of
    past events, structural features, or prior occupants is relevant to
    the sale or valuation
  • Advise on hidden architectural features — including passages, chambers,
    and infrastructure known only to long-term spectral residents
  • Attend buyer viewings on selected premium properties where a ghost
    consultant's personal presence adds historical authenticity
  • Provide reports (dictated to a scribe; we do not expect ghosts to
    manage quill-work) on each property assignment
