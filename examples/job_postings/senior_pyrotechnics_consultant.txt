ABOUT THE ROLE

The Department of Magical Games and Sports is seeking an imaginative and
technically accomplished Senior Pyrotechnics Consultant to lead fireworks
and spectacle design for Ministry ceremonial events, Quidditch World Cup
opening ceremonies, and inter-school magical tournaments.

This is a rare opportunity to work at the highest level of magical
entertainment, applying advanced enchantment design skills to live
large-scale audiences. The successful candidate will join a small but
dedicated team responsible for making the Ministry look good in front of
thousands of people — no pressure.

We are particularly interested in candidates with experience designing
fireworks that can hold their own against standard countercharms and do
something genuinely surprising. Our last three opening ceremonies were
described in the Prophet as "adequate." We are hoping to do better than
adequate.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Lead the design and delivery of large-scale pyrotechnic displays for
    Ministry events, national Quidditch fixtures, and diplomatic receptions
  • Develop original enchanted firework sequences with interactive and
    self-guiding properties
  • Advise on technical specifications for outdoor and indoor display
    environments, including low-clearance atrium settings
  • Coordinate with the Law Enforcement Department to ensure compliance
    with Statute of Secrecy requirements for visibility-containment charms
  • Manage junior technicians and casual staff during live events
  • Maintain an up-to-date inventory of pyrotechnic components and manage
    supplier relationships with approved Diagon Alley vendors
  • Respond to last-minute creative briefs with equanimity

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Demonstrable experience designing original enchanted firework products
    or displays, with physical samples or Pensieve recordings welcome
  • Strong proficiency in self-activating and self-guiding enchantment
    design — static displays will not be considered for this level of role
  • Ability to work under the pressure of live events with large audiences
  • Comfortable working alongside junior staff and delegating effectively
  • Basic Broomstick Operator Licence, Class I or above
  • A working knowledge of Ministry safety regulations, or at minimum
    a willingness to read them before the first event

DESIRABLE

  • Experience creating displays that resist Finite Incantatem and
    standard countercharm attempts (for extended display duration)
  • Background in enchantment-based consumer product development
  • Experience with indoor displays where structural integrity of the
    venue must be considered
  • Sense of humour — the role is high-visibility and things occasionally
    do not go entirely to plan

WHAT WE OFFER

  • 38–52 Galleons per week (negotiable for exceptional candidates)
  • Complimentary access to all Ministry Quidditch events
  • Generous Ministry enchantment materials allowance
  • Opportunity to have your work seen by the entire wizarding world at
    major events, including the Quidditch World Cup
  • Civil service pension (Gringotts Gold-Backed Scheme)
  • 25 days annual leave plus all Bank Holidays (magical and Muggle)

Note: This role may require irregular hours including evenings and
weekends in the period leading up to major events. The Ministry regrets
that it cannot accommodate displays that specifically target the offices
of Senior Undersecretary Dolores Umbridge, however requests of this
nature will be noted and filed appreciatively.
