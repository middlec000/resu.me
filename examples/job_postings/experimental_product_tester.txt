ABOUT GAMBOL & JAPES

Gambol & Japes has been making wizarding Britain laugh since 1861. We are
the original wizarding joke shop, and we intend to remain the best.
Following our most successful decade of trading in recent memory, we are
investing in our Research and Development capability and are looking for
our first dedicated Product Development Tester to join the team.

We'll be honest with you: a new competitor has recently opened at number
93 and they are extremely good. We have no shame in saying this. We have
seen their products. They have made us raise our game. This is, in the
long run, good for everyone.

ABOUT THE ROLE

We need someone to sit in a room and, systematically and methodically,
try things that might go wrong. Then tell us exactly how wrong they went,
why, and what would need to change so that they went right instead.

The successful candidate will test new Gambol & Japes product prototypes
at every stage of development — from initial enchantment sketches through
to pre-release final versions — and provide detailed, technically rigorous
feedback that our development witches and wizards can act on. You will
also be responsible for Safety Assessment sign-off on new products prior
to Ministry compliance review.

This is not a glamorous description. The glamour, such as it is, arrives
when a product you helped develop makes a ten-year-old laugh so hard they
fall off a chair. We have found this is sufficient reward for most of the
right candidates.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Test prototype joke products at all stages of development, including
    early-stage products with known fault conditions
  • Document test outcomes in precise technical language: symptom
    duration, intensity, reversibility, side-effect profile, and
    deviation from intended design
  • Identify failure modes and communicate them clearly to the R&D team
    in actionable terms
  • Conduct consumer simulation testing — i.e., eat, hold, activate, or
    otherwise deploy the product as a real customer would, not as
    someone who knows what's coming
  • Sign off safety assessments for products prior to Ministry compliance
    submission
  • Maintain a personal health log for occupational monitoring purposes
    (mandatory — this is not optional)
  • Participate in product development meetings to provide tester
    perspective on prototype designs before construction

ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Genuine willingness to test products on yourself. This sounds obvious
    but many candidates discover at interview that they didn't quite mean
    it when they said yes in the application. Please be honest with
    yourself before applying.
  • A working knowledge of enchantment design principles — not enough to
    build products yourself, but enough to understand why something did
    what it did and describe it accurately
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Resilience. Physical and psychological.
  • Good general health — we will ask for a St Mungo's baseline
    assessment before appointment and we will also pay for it
  • A sense of humour. Non-negotiable. We tried it once without and
    it was a very quiet six months.

DESIRABLE

  • Prior experience testing magical consumer products in a professional
    or semi-professional capacity — particularly self-administered testing
  • Knowledge of standard antidote and reversal procedure for common
    joke product effects (nosebleed reversal, fainting recovery,
    swelling reduction, etc.)
  • Personal connections to or experience with the Hogwarts-age test
    demographic — our core market is 11–17 and keeping them in mind helps
  • Cheerful outlook in adverse physical circumstances
  • Fast recovery time

A NOTE ON SAFETY

We take the safety of our staff seriously. All test sessions are conducted
with at least one qualified Healer or Potions-competent colleague present.
We maintain a fully stocked antidote cabinet and a direct Floo connection
to St Mungo's emergency intake. We have used it exactly three times in
eleven years and on all three occasions the tester in question returned to
work within the week. We are committed to providing a working environment
where things only go wrong in the ways we expect them to, most of the time.
